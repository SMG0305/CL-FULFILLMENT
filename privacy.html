This notice does not cover information you give us outside this website — for example, in a direct email exchange, in a signed agreement, or during the course of a business relationship — although we handle that information consistently with the principles described here.

12. State privacy rights

We want to be straightforward about how the law applies here rather than reciting requirements that do not fit our business.

Arizona, where we are based, has not enacted a comprehensive consumer data privacy law. Its principal privacy statutes govern data breach notification and the secure disposal of records, both of which we follow.

Roughly twenty other states — including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon, and Nebraska — have comprehensive consumer privacy laws. Most apply only to businesses above stated revenue or consumer-volume thresholds, and the Texas and Nebraska laws exempt small businesses as defined by the U.S. Small Business Administration. CL Fulfillment falls below these thresholds and qualifies as a small business, so most of these laws do not currently impose obligations on us.

We extend the rights listed in Section 11 to every visitor, in every state, as a matter of practice rather than obligation. If our business grows past a threshold that makes one of these laws apply to us, we will update this notice and add whatever additional disclosures that law requires.

For California residents specifically: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act, we do not process sensitive personal information for purposes requiring a right to limit, and we do not use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is accordingly no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link on this site, because there is nothing to opt out of. You may still exercise the access, correction, and deletion rights above, and you may use an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf.
