Where We Stop

What We Don’t Do.

The fastest way to know whether we’re a fit is to know where we stop.

✗ We don’t rebrand your product.

It goes to market as yours — your name, your story, your reputation. We’re not building our own label on the back of your work.

✗ We don’t ask for money up front.

No setup fee, no retainer, no onboarding investment. If we’re buying, you get paid before we’ve sold a single unit.

✗ We don’t sell to liquidators.

Nothing we take on ends up on a clearance site undercutting your own pricing or cheapening what you built.

✗ We don’t take work we can’t do well.

If your product isn’t a fit for online marketplaces, we’ll tell you in the first conversation rather than sign you up anyway.

Questions

The Things Owners Actually Ask.

Straight answers to the questions that decide whether this works for you.

What does it cost me to start?

Nothing. If we’re buying, we pay you for inventory before anything sells. If we’re running your accounts, the fee is agreed in writing before we begin.

Who owns the listings if we part ways?

The listings are yours. If we still hold inventory we bought from you, you can buy it back at exactly what we paid; once it’s sold through, there’s nothing left to settle. Either way, the listings go with you.

What happens to inventory you bought that doesn’t sell?

Once we buy it, it’s our risk. You’ve already been paid. We don’t come back for a refund, a discount, or a credit.

How do I end the agreement?

Either side can end it with written notice, with a 30-day wind-down so open orders get fulfilled. No auto-renewal, no exit fee.

Where does my product ship from?

Your warehouse or fulfillment center. Everything can stay exactly as it is — or if you’d rather look at other options, we’ll evaluate them with you.

Who handles returns and customer service?

Either of us, whatever your team needs. Some partners want us to own marketplace customer service and returns outright; others would rather keep it in-house. We set it up the way that fits.

How long until we’re live?

Most products launch within 180 days of a signed agreement. Photography and listing copy are usually the long pole — if you already have good product photos, it moves faster.

Do you need exclusivity?

Only on the channels we operate.
